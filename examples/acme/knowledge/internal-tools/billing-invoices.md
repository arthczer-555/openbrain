# Invoices in Acme Billing

## When invoices are issued

Subscriptions are billed in advance. Acme Billing issues the invoices automatically:

- Monthly billing cycle: on the first day of each month.
- Annual billing cycle: on the first day of each contract year.

Payment terms are 30 days from the invoice date.

## Overdue invoices

Acme Billing sends reminders to the billing contact 7, 15 and 30 days after the due date. After that, the finance team calls the client and informs the customer success manager. Overdue invoices also lower the score of the client in [client health](hub-client-health.md).

## Send an invoice again

Open **Invoices**, select the invoice and click **Resend**. You can add another email address for this sending only.

## Correct an invoice

An issued invoice is never edited. Issue a credit note for the wrong invoice ([Credit notes in Acme Billing](billing-credit-notes.md)), then a new invoice with the right amounts.
