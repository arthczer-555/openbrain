# Credit notes in Acme Billing

A credit note cancels all or part of an invoice. It is how service credits, billing errors and commercial gestures reach the client. When a client is entitled to a credit is decided by the [refunds and service credits policy](../support-ops/refunds-and-credits.md); this page covers the tool.

## Create a credit note

1. Open the invoice concerned and click **Create credit note**.
2. Pick the **Reason**: Service credit, Billing error or Commercial gesture.
3. Enter the amount and a short note that the client will see.
4. Click **Submit**.

## Approval

A billing editor issues credit notes up to 1,000 EUR at once. Above 1,000 EUR, the credit note waits in **Pending approval** until a finance admin approves it.

## How the client gets the money back

A credit note is deducted from the next invoice. The amount is paid back to the bank account or card of the client only when it corrects a billing error that was already paid, or when the client has left and no future invoice is expected.
