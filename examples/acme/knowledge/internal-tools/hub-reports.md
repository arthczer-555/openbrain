# Weekly reports in Acme Hub

The **Reports** module gathers the weekly figures of the company. These are internal reports about Acme's business, not the reports clients see in Acme Desk.

## The weekly report

Every Monday at 8:00 (Paris time), Acme Hub generates the weekly report and emails it to the leadership team. It covers:

- New business signed during the week, by plan.
- Clients lost and cancellations received.
- Pipeline by stage, from the [Acme Hub pipeline](hub-pipeline.md).
- Support volume and first response time of Acme support.
- Number of clients in red in [client health](hub-client-health.md).

## Who can see the module

The **Reports** module is visible to accounts with the Admin role. Members do not see it (see [Acme Hub accounts](accounts.md)).

## Exports

Each report can be downloaded as a CSV file from **Reports** then **Export**. Exports contain client names: keep them in the company drive.
