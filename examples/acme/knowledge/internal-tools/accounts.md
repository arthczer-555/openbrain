# Acme Hub accounts: give a colleague access

An account in Acme Hub is the login of an Acme employee. Client users never have an account here.

## Add a colleague

Only a Hub admin can add an account. Go to **Settings** then **Team**, click **Add teammate** and enter the colleague's work email. The colleague logs in with Google on first visit.

## Roles

- **Member**: sees pipeline and client health.
- **Admin**: also sees reports, and manages accounts and settings.

## Deactivate an account

When someone leaves, deactivate the account from **Settings** then **Team**. Their deals stay assigned until reassigned.
