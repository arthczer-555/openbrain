# Acme Hub overview

Acme Hub is the internal tool Acme employees use every day. Employees log in with their Google account. Clients never see it.

## Modules

- **Pipeline**: deals and next steps ([Acme Hub pipeline](hub-pipeline.md)).
- **Client health**: usage and risk signals per client company ([Client health in Acme Hub](hub-client-health.md)).
- **Reports**: weekly figures for the leadership team ([Weekly reports in Acme Hub](hub-reports.md)).

## Getting access

A Hub admin creates your account: see [Acme Hub accounts](accounts.md). Invoices and credit notes are not in Acme Hub but in a separate tool, Acme Billing ([Acme Billing overview and access](billing-overview.md)).
