# Acme Billing overview and access

Acme Billing is the internal tool the finance team uses to bill clients: billing accounts, subscriptions, invoices and credit notes. Employees log in with their Google account. Clients never see it.

## Roles

| Role | For whom | Can do |
|---|---|---|
| Viewer | Account executives, customer success managers | See billing accounts, invoices and payment status |
| Billing editor | Finance team | Create billing accounts, issue invoices, draft credit notes |
| Finance admin | Finance team lead | Approve credit notes above 1,000 EUR, manage members |

## Give a colleague access

Only a finance admin can add a colleague. Open **Admin** then **Members**, click **Invite member**, enter the colleague's work email and pick the role. To ask for access, write to the finance mailbox and say which role the job needs.

## Remove access

When someone leaves or changes job, the finance admin removes them from **Admin** then **Members** on their last day.
