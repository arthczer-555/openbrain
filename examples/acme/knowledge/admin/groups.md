# Groups: create and configure

A group is a team of client users who share an inbox, for example "Billing support" or "France".

## Create a group

Open the company in Acme Console, then **Groups** and **New group**. Give it a name and add client users. A client user can belong to several groups.

## Channels and SLA of a group

The **Channels** selector only lists the channels allowed for the company. To offer live chat to a group, first add live chat to the allowed channels of the company (see [Companies: create and manage](companies.md)), then enable it on the group.

The **SLA policy** selector lists the SLA policies of the company, and **Business hours** sets the days and hours when the SLA clock runs for this group. See [Set up SLA policies and business hours](sla-policies.md).
