# Client offboarding

Use this page when a client has cancelled and its contract is ending. The cancellation rules (notice, end date) are in [Renewals and cancellations](../sales/renewals-and-cancellations.md).

## The order that works

1. Confirm the end date of the contract with the account executive.
2. Offer the client a data export before the end date ([Data export](../product/data-export.md)). A full export with attachments takes up to 10 business days, so ask early.
3. On the end date, close the company in Acme Console (**Edit company** then **Close company**). Closing deactivates all client users.
4. Tell the finance team, who issue the final invoice and close the billing account in Acme Billing ([Billing accounts in Acme Billing](../internal-tools/billing-accounts.md)).
5. Mark the client as lost in Acme Hub, so that it leaves the client health view.

## Who does each step

The customer success manager runs steps one, two, three and five. The finance team runs step four.

## What happens to the data

Tickets follow the retention rules of the product ([Data retention in Acme Desk](../product/data-retention.md)). If the client wants its tickets deleted earlier, a client admin must ask for it in writing through support before the end date.
