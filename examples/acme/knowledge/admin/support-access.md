# Open a client workspace as support

Acme staff cannot see the tickets of a client by default. To look at the workspace of a client, for example to reproduce a problem, support needs a support access granted by the client.

## The client grants access

A client admin opens **Settings** then **Security** in Acme Desk and clicks **Grant support access**. Access lasts 72 hours by default and can be extended up to 7 days. The client admin can revoke it at any time.

## Open the workspace

In Acme Console, open the company and click **Open as support**. The button is greyed out when no access is active. Every session is logged (staff member, time, pages opened) and client admins can read this log in Acme Desk.

## Rules during the session

- Look only at what the request needs. Never export or copy tickets.
- Do not change settings for the client: describe the change and let a client admin make it, unless the client asks for it in writing in the ticket.
- Close the session when you are done.
