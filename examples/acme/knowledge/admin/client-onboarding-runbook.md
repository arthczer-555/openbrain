# Client onboarding runbook

Use this runbook for every newly signed client. It gives the order of the steps; the fields of each screen are described on the pages linked below.

## Before you start

Read the handover note written by the account executive (scope, channels, number of agents, key contacts). Check that the finance team has created the billing account of the client in Acme Billing ([Billing accounts in Acme Billing](../internal-tools/billing-accounts.md)).

## The order that works

1. Create the company with its plan, agent seats and allowed channels ([Companies](companies.md)).
2. Create the SLA policies of the company ([Set up SLA policies and business hours](sla-policies.md)).
3. Create the groups and pick their channels, business hours and SLA policy ([Groups](groups.md)).
4. Import the client users ([Client users](users.md)).
5. Name one or two company admins on the company page.
6. Enterprise plan only: set up single sign-on with the IT team of the client ([Single sign-on](sso-setup.md)).
7. Book the launch meeting with the client.

## Who does each step

The account executive hands over to the customer success manager, who runs steps one to six and leads the launch meeting. See [Account roles and handover](../sales/account-roles-and-handover.md).
