# Remote work policy

## The rule

Employees can work remotely up to three days a week. Team rituals on Tuesday stay in person. Agree on your office days with your manager and keep them visible in the team calendar.

## Exceptions

Your manager approves temporary exceptions. Working from abroad for more than two weeks needs HR approval first.

## Working from home safely

Your laptop and accessories are provided by Acme ([Laptop and equipment](equipment.md)). Follow the [Information security policy for employees](../legal-security/security-policy.md), in particular when you work from a public place.
