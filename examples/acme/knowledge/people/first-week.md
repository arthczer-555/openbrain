# Your first week at Acme

A checklist for new employees. Your manager and your onboarding buddy help you through it.

## Day one

- Collect your laptop from IT and log in with your Google account.
- Turn on two-factor authentication on your Google account ([Information security policy for employees](../legal-security/security-policy.md)).
- Open the HR portal and check your personal and bank details.
- Log in to Acme Hub: a Hub admin has created your account before your arrival ([Acme Hub accounts](../internal-tools/accounts.md)).

## During the week

- Meet your onboarding buddy, a colleague from another team who answers practical questions.
- Read the [Remote work policy](remote-work.md) and agree on your office days with your manager.
- If your job needs Acme Billing (finance, account executives, customer success), ask for access through the finance mailbox: a finance admin adds you ([Acme Billing overview and access](../internal-tools/billing-overview.md)).
- Get a demo of Acme Desk from the customer success team.

## Within two weeks

Complete the security training in the training platform. It is mandatory for every employee.
