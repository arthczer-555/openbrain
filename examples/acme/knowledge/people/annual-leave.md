# Annual leave and public holidays

## How many days

Full-time employees get 25 working days of paid leave per year, plus the public holidays of their country of contract. Part-time employees get the same number of weeks, converted into days.

## Request leave

1. Open the HR portal, then **Time off** and **New request**.
2. Pick the dates and the type **Paid leave**.
3. Your manager approves or declines in the HR portal. You get an email either way.

Ask at least two weeks in advance for three days or more. For a single day, a few days of notice are enough. Check the team calendar first: each team keeps at least one person available on working days.

## Carry-over

You can carry up to 5 unused days into the next year. Carried-over days must be taken before 31 March, otherwise they are lost.

## Questions

Payroll and leave questions go to the HR operations mailbox ([HR contacts](hr-contacts.md)).
