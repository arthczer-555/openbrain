# Sick leave: what to do

## On the first day

Tell your manager before the start of your working day, by message or phone. You do not have to say what illness you have.

## In the HR portal

Record the absence the same day in the HR portal, under **Time off**, with the type **Sick leave**. If you cannot do it yourself, your manager records it for you.

## Medical certificate

For an absence longer than two working days, upload a medical certificate in the HR portal within 48 hours. Only the HR team sees the certificate.

## Coming back

Update the end date in the HR portal if you come back earlier or later than planned. After an absence of more than four weeks, the HR team offers a return-to-work meeting.

Questions go to the HR operations mailbox ([HR contacts](hr-contacts.md)).
