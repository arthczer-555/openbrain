# Laptop and equipment

## What you get

Every employee gets a laptop and a headset, and a desk with a screen on office days. On request, IT also provides one external screen, a keyboard and a mouse for home.

## Renewal

Laptops are renewed every three years, or earlier if they break. Ask the IT mailbox: IT sends the new laptop and collects the old one.

## Lost or stolen laptop

Report it at once to the security mailbox, as a security incident ([Security incidents and data breaches](../legal-security/security-incidents.md)), then to the IT mailbox. IT locks and wipes the laptop remotely.

## Small purchases

Equipment up to 100 EUR can be bought and claimed with the approval of your manager ([Expense policy](expenses.md)).

## Leaving Acme

Return the laptop and accessories to IT on your last day.
