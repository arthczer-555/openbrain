# Business travel

## Before you book

Your manager approves the trip before anything is booked. Book through the travel booking tool on the intranet: it applies the rules below and sends the invoices to the finance team directly.

## Transport

- Take the train when the journey is under four hours.
- Flights are in economy class.
- Book at least two weeks ahead when you can: late bookings cost much more.

## Hotels

| City | Maximum per night, breakfast included |
|---|---|
| Capital cities | 200 EUR |
| Other cities | 150 EUR |

## During the trip

Meals and taxis follow the [Expense policy](expenses.md). Keep every receipt.

## Visiting a client

Tell the customer success manager or the account executive in charge of the client before the visit, so that it is logged in Acme Hub.
