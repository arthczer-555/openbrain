# HR contacts: who to ask

- **Payroll and leave**: the HR operations mailbox.
- **Contracts and policies**: the people partner of your team.
- **Wellbeing**: the confidential support line listed on the intranet.
- **Expense claims**: the finance mailbox ([Expense policy](expenses.md)).
- **Laptop and equipment**: the IT mailbox ([Laptop and equipment](equipment.md)).
- **A request about your own personal data** (access, correction): the privacy mailbox ([Data subject requests](../legal-security/data-subject-requests.md)).

If your question is not covered by the People & HR section, ask the HR operations mailbox first.
