# Expense policy

## What you can claim

| Expense | Rule |
|---|---|
| Train, plane and hotel for work | Booked as described in [Business travel](business-travel.md) |
| Meals during a business trip | Up to 25 EUR per meal |
| Meals with a client or prospect | Up to 60 EUR per person, approved by your manager before the meal |
| Taxi | Only when no public transport is available, or after 22:00 |
| Small equipment | Up to 100 EUR, approved by your manager; above that, ask IT ([Laptop and equipment](equipment.md)) |

Commuting between home and the office is not an expense.

## Submit a claim

1. Open the expense tool from the intranet and click **New expense**.
2. Add a photo of the receipt, the amount, the date and the reason.
3. Submit within 30 days of the expense. Claims older than 30 days are refused.

Your manager approves the claim, then the finance team reimburses it with the next payroll.

## Questions

Write to the finance mailbox.
