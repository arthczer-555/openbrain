# Plans and features

Acme Desk has three plans: Starter, Growth and Enterprise. The plan is set on the company and applies to every group of the company. What each plan costs is on the [Pricing](../sales/pricing.md) page.

## What each plan includes

| Feature | Starter | Growth | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email channel | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Live chat channel | No | Yes | Yes |
| Phone channel | No | No | Yes |
| SLA policies | One SLA policy | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Reports | No | Yes | Yes |
| API and webhooks | No | Yes | Yes |
| Single sign-on (SSO) | No | No | Yes |
| Uptime commitment | No | Yes | Yes |
| Phone support from Acme | No | No | Yes |
| Dedicated success manager | No | No | Yes |

Data export, two-factor authentication, macros and automation rules are available on every plan. A free trial gives the Growth features for a limited time ([Free trial](../sales/free-trial.md)).

## What the plan caps

The plan is the top of the chain: a company can only allow the channels of its plan, and a group can only use the channels allowed for its company. The same goes for SLA policies: on Starter, all groups share the single SLA policy of the company.

## Changing plan

A client changes plan through its account executive. The steps are in [Plan and seat changes](../internal-tools/billing-plan-changes.md).
