# Ticket channels

## Available channels

Acme Desk receives tickets from three channels: email, live chat and phone (call transcripts are attached to the ticket).

## Why a channel does not appear for a group

A group can only use the channels allowed for its company. The list of allowed channels is set in Acme Console on the company page (field **Allowed channels**). If a client asks for live chat on a group and the option is greyed out, the company does not allow live chat yet: it must be added at company level first. See [Companies: create and manage](../admin/companies.md).

A company can only allow the channels of its plan: live chat needs Growth or Enterprise, phone needs Enterprise ([Plans and features](plans-and-features.md)).
