# Acme Desk overview and roles

Acme Desk is the helpdesk app our clients use to answer their own customers. Each client company works in its own workspace. What a company can use depends on its plan ([Plans and features](plans-and-features.md)).

## Roles

| Role | Can do | Cannot do |
|---|---|---|
| Agent | Answer tickets assigned to their groups, add internal notes | Change SLA policies, see other groups |
| Supervisor | Everything an agent does, reassign tickets, see group reports | Change company settings |
| Client admin | Manage groups and client users of the company, see all reports | Enable a channel that is not allowed for the company |

## Main screens

- **Inbox**: the tickets of the groups the user belongs to ([Inbox and ticket statuses](ticket-statuses.md)).
- **Reports**: volumes, first response time, SLA compliance, on the Growth and Enterprise plans ([Reports in Acme Desk](reports.md)).
- **Settings**: only visible to client admins.
