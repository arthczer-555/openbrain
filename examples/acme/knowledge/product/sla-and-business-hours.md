# SLA policies and business hours

## How the clock works

An SLA policy defines a first response target and a resolution target per priority. The clock only runs during the business hours of the group. Holidays set on the company pause every group of that company. The clock is also paused while a ticket is **Pending** ([Inbox and ticket statuses](ticket-statuses.md)).

## Breaches

A ticket that misses its target is flagged in red in the inbox and counted in the SLA compliance report. Supervisors receive a daily digest of breached tickets.

## Setting them up

SLA policies and business hours are created in Acme Console during onboarding, then client admins can change them: see [Set up SLA policies and business hours](../admin/sla-policies.md).
