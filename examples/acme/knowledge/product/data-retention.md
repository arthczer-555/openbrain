# Data retention in Acme Desk

## Tickets

Closed tickets are kept for 24 months after closure, then deleted automatically, attachments included.

## Call transcripts

Phone transcripts follow the same rule as their ticket.

## Deletion on request

A client admin can ask for the deletion of a ticket before the end of the period through support.

## Keeping tickets longer

Tickets cannot be kept beyond the period. A client that wants its own copy exports its tickets before they are deleted ([Data export](data-export.md)).
