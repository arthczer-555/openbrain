# Reports in Acme Desk

The **Reports** screen shows the activity of the support team of a client. It is available on the Growth and Enterprise plans. These are reports for clients, not the internal reports of Acme Hub.

## Available reports

| Report | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Volume | Tickets created and solved, by channel and by group |
| First response time | Median time to the first answer, in business hours |
| Resolution time | Median time from creation to solved |
| SLA compliance | Share of tickets that met their SLA targets |
| Tags | Tickets per tag |

## Who sees what

Supervisors see the reports of their groups. Client admins see the reports of every group of the company. Agents do not see reports.

## Export

Every report can be exported as a CSV file with **Export**. To export the tickets themselves, see [Data export](data-export.md).
