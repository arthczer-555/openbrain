# Uptime commitment to clients

What Acme commits to on the availability of Acme Desk. This is the commitment of Acme to its clients, not the SLA policies that clients set for their own customers in Acme Desk.

## The commitment

| Plan | Monthly availability |
|---|---|
| Starter | No commitment (best effort) |
| Growth | 99.9% |
| Enterprise | 99.9% |

Availability is measured every minute by an external monitoring service, and the monthly figure is published on the status page.

## Planned maintenance

Planned maintenance does not count as downtime when it is announced on the status page at least 5 days in advance. It is scheduled outside [Acme support hours](support-hours.md) and lasts at most 4 hours per month.

## If we miss it

A client on Growth or Enterprise can ask for a service credit: see [Refunds and service credits](refunds-and-credits.md).
