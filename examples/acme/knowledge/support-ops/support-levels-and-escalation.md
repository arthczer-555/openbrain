# Support levels and escalation

How the support team of Acme is organised to answer clients. The helpdesk features that Acme Desk sells are in the Product section.

## Levels

| Level | Who | Handles |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Support agents | Every request from client admins and client users: how-to, settings, access |
| Level 2 | Product specialists | Complex configuration, full data exports, bugs to reproduce |
| Engineering | On-call engineer | P1 and P2 incidents, bugs confirmed by level 2 |

## When level 1 escalates to level 2

- The request is not solved within one business day.
- A bug is suspected.
- The fix needs a change in Acme Console that level 1 cannot make, or a full data export.

Escalate by assigning the ticket to the **Product specialists** group, with a short summary.

## When engineering is called

Level 2 files bugs for engineering ([Bug reports and feature requests](bug-reports-and-feature-requests.md)). For a P1 incident, anyone in support pages the on-call engineer at once ([Engineering on-call](on-call.md), [Incident severity levels](incident-severity.md)).

## Who talks to the client

The level 1 agent who took the request stays the contact of the client until it is solved, even while level 2 or engineering work on it.
