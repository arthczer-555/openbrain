# Acme support hours and response targets

When the support team of Acme answers clients, and how fast. These are the hours of Acme support, not the business hours a client sets for its groups in Acme Desk.

## Hours and channels

Acme support answers by email and live chat, Monday to Friday, from 8:00 to 19:00 Paris time, except French public holidays. Enterprise clients can also call the support phone line during the same hours.

P1 incidents are handled at any time, for every plan: the on-call engineer is paged ([Engineering on-call](on-call.md)).

## First response targets

| Plan | First response from Acme support |
|---|---|
| Starter | Within 1 business day |
| Growth | Within 8 support hours |
| Enterprise | Within 4 support hours |

Support hours only count the hours above: a request sent on Friday evening is answered on Monday.

## Who can contact support

Client admins and client users of a client company. Customers of our clients are sent back to the client; a request about their personal data follows [Data subject requests](../legal-security/data-subject-requests.md).
