# Proposals and order forms

## The proposal

Start from the proposal template in the sales drive. Fill in the plan, the number of agent seats, the billing cycle and the start date. Prices and discount rules come from [Pricing](pricing.md): never promise a discount that has not been approved.

## The order form

The order form is the contract. It is generated from the proposal and signed through the e-signature tool. Every order form includes:

- the standard terms of service,
- the standard data processing agreement ([Data processing agreement (DPA) process](../legal-security/dpa-process.md)),
- the plan, the seats, the billing cycle and the start date.

## Non-standard terms

If the client asks to change the standard terms (liability, payment terms, its own contract template), send the request to the legal team through the legal request form before agreeing to anything. Legal answers within 5 business days. A DPA sent by the client follows the DPA process instead.

## Amendments

During an annual contract, a change of plan or of the number of seats needs a signed order form amendment, made from the same template. The rest of the change follows [Plan and seat changes](../internal-tools/billing-plan-changes.md).

## After signature

Move the deal to **Closed won** in Acme Hub and write the handover note ([Account roles and handover](account-roles-and-handover.md)). The finance team creates the billing account from the signed order form.
