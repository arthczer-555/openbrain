# Renewals and cancellations

## Annual contracts

Annual contracts renew automatically for one more year, unless the client cancels in writing at least 60 days before the end date. The customer success manager starts the renewal conversation 90 days before the end date; Acme Hub creates a reminder task.

A price change at renewal must be approved by the head of sales and announced to the client at least 90 days before the end date.

## Monthly contracts

A monthly contract can be cancelled at any time. The cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month; the month already invoiced is not refunded.

## When a client cancels

1. Acknowledge the cancellation in writing within two business days and confirm the end date.
2. Log it in Acme Hub on the client page, with the reason.
3. Offer a call to understand the reason; the account executive joins for clients on the Enterprise plan.
4. Start the [Client offboarding](../admin/client-offboarding.md), so that the client can export its data before the end date.

## Who handles it

The customer success manager in charge of the client, with the account executive for any commercial discussion.
