# Pricing

## Plans

| Plan | Price per agent seat per month | Included |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 19 EUR | Email channel, one SLA policy |
| Growth | 39 EUR | Email and live chat, unlimited SLA policies, reports |
| Enterprise | 59 EUR (to be confirmed) | All channels including phone, SSO, dedicated success manager |

The full comparison of features is in [Plans and features](../product/plans-and-features.md).

## Discounts

Annual billing gives two months free. Any other discount needs the approval of the head of sales.
