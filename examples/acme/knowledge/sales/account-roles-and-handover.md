# Account roles and handover

## Roles

- **Account executive (AE)**: signs the deal and writes the handover note.
- **Customer success manager (CSM)**: owns the setup of the client in Acme Console and the launch meeting.
- **Support**: answers client questions once the client is live ([Support levels and escalation](../support-ops/support-levels-and-escalation.md)).
- **Finance**: creates the billing account and sends the invoices ([Billing accounts in Acme Billing](../internal-tools/billing-accounts.md)).

## The handover

Once a client has signed, the AE shares the handover note within two days of signature (scope, channels, number of agents, key contacts) and introduces the CSM to the client. The CSM then sets the client up with the [Client onboarding runbook](../admin/client-onboarding-runbook.md).

## After the launch

Enterprise clients keep a dedicated CSM. On the other plans, the CSM steps back after the launch and support becomes the first contact of the client; the CSM still handles the renewal ([Renewals and cancellations](renewals-and-cancellations.md)).
