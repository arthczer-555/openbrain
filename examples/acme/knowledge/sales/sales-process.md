# Sales process and deal stages

Every deal follows the same stages. The stage of each deal is kept up to date in the [Acme Hub pipeline](../internal-tools/hub-pipeline.md).

## Stages

| Stage | The deal moves on when |
|---|---|
| Qualified | The prospect has a helpdesk need, a budget and a decision date |
| Demo | A demo of Acme Desk has been given to the people who will decide |
| Trial | The prospect tests Acme Desk ([Free trial](free-trial.md)); optional for small deals |
| Proposal | A written proposal has been sent ([Proposals and order forms](proposals-and-order-forms.md)) |
| Negotiation | The prospect discusses terms or price |
| Closed won | The order form is signed |
| Closed lost | The prospect said no, or stopped answering for a month |

## Weekly pipeline review

Every Monday morning, the sales team reviews the deals in **Proposal** and **Negotiation** with the head of sales. Update your deals in Acme Hub before the meeting.

## After closed won

The account executive writes the handover note and introduces the customer success manager ([Account roles and handover](account-roles-and-handover.md)).
