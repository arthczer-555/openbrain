# Free trial

## What the trial includes

A free trial lasts 14 days and gives the features of the Growth plan, for up to 5 agent seats. No payment details are asked.

## Start a trial

The account executive creates the trial company in Acme Console: **Companies**, **New company**, plan **Growth** and the **Trial** box ticked. Then invite the first users of the prospect ([Client users](../admin/users.md)). For a guided demo, use the [demo workspace](demo-workspace.md) instead.

## Extend a trial

A trial can be extended once, by 7 days, with the approval of the head of sales.

## At the end of the trial

- **The prospect signs**: the customer success manager unticks **Trial**, sets the plan signed and the agent seats. Tickets and settings of the trial are kept.
- **The prospect does not sign**: the company is blocked at the end of the trial, then closed 30 days later.
