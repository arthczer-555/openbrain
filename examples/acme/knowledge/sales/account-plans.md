# Account plans for strategic clients

An account plan is a short document that describes how we keep and grow a strategic client. It is a sales document, not a subscription plan: for the plans of Acme Desk, see [Plans and features](../product/plans-and-features.md).

## Which clients need one

Every client on the Enterprise plan, and every client with more than 100 agent seats.

## What it contains

Use the account plan template in Notion, one page per client:

- **Goals**: what the client wants to achieve with Acme Desk this year.
- **Stakeholders**: who decides, who uses, who pays, and our relationship with each.
- **Risks**: signals from [client health](../internal-tools/hub-client-health.md), open escalations, renewal date.
- **Growth**: new teams, channels or seats that could come next.
- **Next actions**: an owner and a date for each.

## Who writes it and when

The account executive and the customer success manager write it together within a month of signature, and review it every quarter. The head of sales reviews account plans before each renewal.
