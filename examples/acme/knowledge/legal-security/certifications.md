# Certifications and audits

## ISO 27001

Acme is preparing the ISO 27001 certification. The project is in progress: the information security management system is being documented and the certification audit has not taken place yet. Until the certificate is issued, never tell a client or a prospect that Acme is ISO 27001 certified. You can say that the certification is in progress.

## Penetration test

An external company runs a penetration test of Acme Desk every year. The summary report can be shared with a client or a prospect under NDA, through the security team.

## Other certifications

There is no other certification project at the moment. If a client requires a specific certification, tell the security team, who records the request.

## Client audits

Enterprise clients can audit Acme once a year, by questionnaire first. On-site audits are agreed case by case with the security and legal teams.
