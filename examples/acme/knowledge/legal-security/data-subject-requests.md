# Data subject requests

A data subject request is a person asking to access, correct or delete their personal data.

## From a customer of a client

People who wrote to a client through Acme Desk sometimes contact Acme directly. For this data, Acme is a processor and the client decides ([GDPR at Acme: roles and principles](gdpr-overview.md)).

1. Do not answer on the substance and do not delete anything.
2. Forward the request to the client admins of the company concerned within 2 business days.
3. Tell the person that their request was forwarded to the company they contacted.

If the client then asks Acme to delete specific tickets before the end of the retention period, a client admin sends that request through support ([Data retention in Acme Desk](../product/data-retention.md)).

## About data Acme controls

For employees, candidates and contacts of clients and prospects, Acme is the controller. Forward the request to the privacy mailbox the same day. Legal answers within one month.

## Keep a trace

Every request, and the date it was forwarded, is logged in the privacy register: copy the privacy mailbox on your forward.
