# Subprocessors

A subprocessor (also written sub-processor) is a company that processes client data on behalf of Acme, for example our hosting provider.

## Categories

| Category | What it does | Location of data |
|---|---|---|
| Cloud hosting | Hosts Acme Desk and its databases | European Union |
| Email delivery | Sends and receives ticket emails | European Union |
| Telephony and transcription | Carries phone calls and transcribes them (phone channel) | European Union |
| Error monitoring | Collects technical errors, with personal data masked | European Union |

## The list with names

The list of subprocessors, with their names, is not public. It is sent on request with the data processing agreement ([Data processing agreement (DPA) process](dpa-process.md)). Ask the legal team for the current version; never send an old copy.

## Adding a subprocessor

Clients are told at least 30 days before a new subprocessor starts processing their data. A client can object in writing during that period; legal handles objections.
