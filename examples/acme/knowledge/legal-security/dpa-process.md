# Data processing agreement (DPA) process

A data processing agreement (DPA) sets how Acme processes the personal data of a client in Acme Desk.

## Standard DPA

The standard DPA of Acme is attached to every order form ([Proposals and order forms](../sales/proposals-and-order-forms.md)). In most deals nothing else is needed: the DPA is signed with the order form.

## When the client sends its own DPA

1. Do not sign it and do not promise anything.
2. Send it to the legal team through the legal request form, with the draft order form and the plan.
3. Legal reviews it within 10 business days and sends back comments or a signed version.
4. If legal cannot accept a clause, the account executive and legal discuss it with the client.

## Where signed DPAs are kept

Legal stores every signed DPA with the order form, in the contract folder of the client. Ask legal for a copy; never send a draft to the client from your own drive.

## Questions clients often ask

- The list of subprocessors is sent on request with the DPA ([Subprocessors](subprocessors.md)).
- For retention, the reference is [Data retention in Acme Desk](../product/data-retention.md).
