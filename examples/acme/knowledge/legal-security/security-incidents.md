# Security incidents and data breaches

A security incident is anything that may expose data or access: a lost or stolen laptop, a phishing email you clicked, data sent to the wrong person, a suspected intrusion. Service outages are handled by support: see [Incident communication](../support-ops/incident-communication.md).

## Report it

Write to the security mailbox at once, even if you are not sure. Say what happened, when, and which data or tools may be concerned. Do not investigate alone and do not delete anything.

## What the security team does

1. Assesses the incident and decides whether personal data is at risk.
2. Contains it: locks accounts, wipes devices, revokes keys.
3. Logs it in the incident register, with the actions taken.

## When client data is concerned

If a breach of personal data affects client data in Acme Desk, the security team and legal notify the client admins of the affected companies within 48 hours of confirming it, so that clients can meet their own legal deadlines. Support and customer success never contact clients about a breach on their own: they use the message prepared by legal.

## Afterwards

The security team shares a summary with the leadership team, with the actions that prevent it from happening again.
