# GDPR at Acme: roles and principles

## Our two roles

| Data | Role of Acme | Who decides |
|---|---|---|
| Tickets, messages, attachments and call transcripts in Acme Desk | Processor | The client, who is the controller |
| Login data of client users (email, name) | Processor | The client |
| Employee and candidate data | Controller | Acme |
| Contacts of clients and prospects in Acme Hub and Acme Billing | Controller | Acme |

As a processor, Acme only processes client data on the instructions of the client, as written in the data processing agreement ([Data processing agreement (DPA) process](dpa-process.md)).

## Principles we apply

- **Location**: client data in Acme Desk is hosted in the European Union.
- **Minimum access**: staff see the tickets of a client only with a support access granted by the client ([Open a client workspace as support](../admin/support-access.md)).
- **Retention**: tickets are deleted automatically at the end of the retention period ([Data retention in Acme Desk](../product/data-retention.md)).
- **Subprocessors**: listed, and announced to clients in advance ([Subprocessors](subprocessors.md)).

## Who to ask

Privacy questions go to the privacy mailbox, read by the legal team. Requests from people about their own data follow [Data subject requests](data-subject-requests.md).
