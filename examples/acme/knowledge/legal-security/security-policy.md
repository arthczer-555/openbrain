# Information security policy for employees

Rules every employee follows, in the office, at home and when travelling.

## Accounts and passwords

- Two-factor authentication is mandatory on your Google account and on every internal tool that offers it.
- Use the company password manager. Never reuse a work password elsewhere.
- Never share your login, even with a colleague.

## Devices

- Lock your screen whenever you leave your laptop.
- Install updates within a week of their release.
- In public places, use a privacy screen and never leave the laptop unattended.

## Client data

- Client data stays in Acme Desk, Acme Console, Acme Hub and Acme Billing. Do not copy tickets into documents, emails or other tools.
- Look at the workspace of a client only with a support access ([Open a client workspace as support](../admin/support-access.md)).
- For AI tools, follow [Using AI tools at work (draft)](ai-tools-policy.md).

## Report it at once

A lost laptop, a suspicious email you clicked, data sent to the wrong person: write to the security mailbox right away ([Security incidents and data breaches](security-incidents.md)).

## Training

Every employee completes the security training within two weeks of arrival, then every year.
